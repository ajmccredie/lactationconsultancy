About Aga

Knowledge, calm and practical support.

Feeding support built around your baby, your circumstances and your choices.

Hello, I’m Aga

I combine specialist clinical experience with genuinely personal care.

I am an International Board Certified Lactation Consultant and neonatal nurse with experience supporting premature and term babies, twins and families navigating more complex feeding needs.

I have worked for much of my professional career in the neonatal intensive care unit at St Thomas' Hospital in London, and have years of experience of families with non-straightforward feeding needs and journeys.

My role is not to tell you there is one “right” way to feed. It is to listen carefully, explain what is happening, give you evidence-based options, and help you find a plan that is realistic for you.
